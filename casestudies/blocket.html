My roles at Blocket
  • Senior product designer

I've spent the past seven years at Blocket, working across product, visual, and UX design — contributing to nearly every area and feature across the range of products.

I was also one of the founding members of the design system team, where I helped define design principles, build the component library, and took on a key role in supporting and guiding a team of around 20 designers to create consistent, high-quality user experiences.

Blocket Design System.
As a founding member of Blocket's design system, I collaborated with a small team of developers to implement token structures, enhance the component library, and create clear documentation. Through internal communication, presentations, and advocacy, we achieved strong adoption across the organization.

One of the key lessons I've learned is that successful design systems rely on communication. I have extensive experience giving presentations and running workshops with system users to ensure alignment and shared understanding."
